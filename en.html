Highline courses

In 2019, Slack'icîmes became the first association in France to offer highline courses. Since then, the association has introduced more than 700 people to this dizzying sport. It offers courses in different formats, suitable for every level.

The association

The association was founded in 2012 and has kept slackline and highline alive in Haute Tarentaise, Savoie, in particular through the Winter Festislack, which over 10 editions has brought together 150 practitioners from all over the world at the Deux Têtes, in the Les Arcs ski area.

Two women now lead the association, highly motivated to develop its many facets.

Meet the team

Winter Festislack

A one of a kind event combining skiing and highline. It brings together practitioners from all over the world on the fabulous natural site of the Deux Têtes, in the heart of the Les Arcs ski area, facing Mont Blanc.
