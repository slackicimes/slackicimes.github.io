A typical week

On the program: a little slackline, a lot of highline, a fun day at the lake, and dedicated time for mental preparation and managing fear and emotions.

  • Monday

    At the park

    • Learn the basics and/or progress
    • Highline specific workshops
    • Stretching and breathing exercises
  • Tuesday

    Highline spot

    • Highline practice
    • Fear management exercises
  • Wednesday

    At the lake

    • Waterline (slackline over water)
    • Slackline and balance games
  • Thursday and Friday

    Highline spot

    • Highline practice
    • Emotion management exercises
