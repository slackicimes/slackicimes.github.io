The event

2027 World Cup – First stop

Highline is a modern take on tightrope walking, on a 2.5 centimeter wide elastic webbing rigged high above the ground. Highline freestyle is its acrobatic discipline. Athletes chain rotations, twists and spectacular tricks.

The ISA World Cup Series is the discipline's first world circuit, launched by the International Slackline Association (ISA). The Les Arcs event is the first stop of the 2027 season.

Format & Origins

The battle

Two athletes start at the same time on two parallel lines and answer each other trick after trick, like a dance battle. The judges decide.

Born in Les Arcs

The battle format was created here, at the Deux Têtes, in 2022. It is now used in almost every world competition, World Championships included.

How does the competition work?

Groups of 4

Each athlete earns a rank in their group, from 1st to 4th.

Athletes

Information for athletes

12 athletes per category: the top 6 of the ISA ranking (after the Poland stop) and 6 video wild cards. If a ranked athlete withdraws, an extra wild card is awarded.

The competition

The setup

  • All lines will be Dyneema, with SlackX adjustable bungees.
  • Mainline: Dynosaur 2, backup: Dienomore, by Sewings El Patrón.
  • Two pairs of lines: two of 68 m and two of 74 m.
  • Each category (Men and Women) will be assigned a fixed length.

Video format

  • Two combos of 7 bounces.
  • The bounce into your first position does not count (rocket start, backbounce…).
  • Show your control after the 7 bounces: 3 bounces in the final position.
  • At the start or end of the combo, make the mandatory sign: a triangle with both hands.

Rules

  • No editing within a combo, a single camera angle.
  • No slow motion.
  • Both combos can be in one or two videos.

Video judging

Difficulty (tier list, a repeated trick drops one tier)
40 pts
Style, control and originality
20 pts / combo
Variety between the two combos
20 pts

Why this format? The competition is played only in battles, with many combos: we want athletes who can land them again and again, and variety to avoid repetitive battles.
