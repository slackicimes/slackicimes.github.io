Haute Tarentaise · Savoie · Since 2012

The association

Founded in 2012, the association has kept slackline and highline alive in Haute Tarentaise, Savoie, notably through the Winter Festislack event, which over 10 editions has brought together 150 practitioners from all over the world on the Les Arcs ski area, at the Deux Têtes.

Two women now lead the association, highly motivated to develop its many facets.

Slack'icîmes is...

The association has always been committed to developing slackline and highline, and to sharing their values.

  • Courses

    In 2019, the association ran the first highline courses in France. Since then, more than 300 people have discovered highline with us.

  • A festival

    Since 2012, the Winter Festislack has brought together athletes from all over the world around a unique concept combining skiing and highline.

  • A competition

    From April 14 to 16, 2027, the association hosts the Highline Freestyle World Cup in Les Arcs, on the main square of Arc 2000.

  • Introductions

    Together with towns and tourist offices, we let children and adults test their balance.

  • Demonstrations

    The association has delighted admiring audiences at many events.

  • A commitment

    We work together with the ISA (International Slackline Association).

The duo

In 2019, Chloë and Gaëlle joined forces to set up the first highline introduction courses in France.

Long involved in the highline community, and passionate about teaching in their original disciplines (skiing for Chloë, trampoline for Gaëlle), they share their experience: helping people of every background take their first steps into the void, and guiding them with kindness along this life changing learning path.

    +–Read the full story: Chloë

    I discovered highline in its very early days in France when I met the Flying Frenchies, pioneers of the sport, and watched the discipline evolve over the years: rigging, gear, techniques…

    The beauty of highline made me want to reach alpine places, so I spent several years climbing and mountaineering to be able to rig the lines I dreamed of.

    Those years of learning brought as much inner work as technical and physical work, and I became passionate about passing on my experience: first for several years as a festival volunteer, then since 2019 through introduction courses with Gaëlle.

    The magic that happens in the highline community is contagious and lasts over the years, and my passion has not aged a bit, unlike me ;)

  • Gaëlle

    Highline instructor & Winter Festislack organizer

    Highlining since2014

    Highline perfectly meets two of my needs: understanding & sharing. It changes how I handle fear, and how I see life.
    +–Read the full story: Gaëlle

    In 2011, slackline came into my life and gradually took up a big place. Already used to cliffs thanks to climbing, I naturally moved on to highline.

    After a difficult start, highline ended up changing me. It changed how I deal with fear, how I see life, and the people around me.

    It perfectly meets two of my needs: understanding & sharing.

    Understanding the technical rigging and the constant evolution of gear, but also fear, pushing yourself, acceptance, and all the other feelings we go through. And sharing my knowledge, seeing the sparkle in the eyes of people discovering slackline, and working with them on what holds them back.

    Since then, I have travelled a lot, spent time in the mountains, rigged highlines at many festivals, and run countless introductions.

    After several years as president of the Grenoble slackline association, I met Chloë, and our shared values combined with our complementary skills gave life to new projects and experiences, which confirm once again that slackline can change lives.

  • Alexian

    Highline freestyle instructor

    Highlining since2016

    I love the intense flow you can reach in this sport, the artistic side of our practice and the passionate community around slackline.
    +–Read the full story: Alexian

    Slackline came into my life when I was 18. I first fell for longline, before moving on to highline.

    My first highline experience was very (too?) emotional. I went home shaking, thinking it was not for me. In the end, surrounded by the right people, I pushed past it, and this passion quickly guided my life.

    I love the intense flow you can reach in this sport, the artistic side of our practice and the passionate community around slackline.

    Highline made me rediscover mountain sports and see my surroundings with new eyes. I love mixing my passion with other mountain activities: climbing, ski touring, mountaineering, maybe paragliding soon…

    Without knowing it, I was always doing “highline freestyle” before the discipline had a name. I have watched it evolve over the years and I hope to share it with as many people as possible. It offers another vision of highline and lets you push yourself at every level!
