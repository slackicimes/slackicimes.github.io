Slackline introduction

Depending on your event and your audience, two formats are possible:

Open access slack park

The public comes and goes freely, and everyone tries the lines at their own pace. Our instructors are there to advise and support.

Lessons

Sessions in small groups, with about one instructor for 10 people. Ideal for schools, holiday clubs and groups.

  • All equipment is provided.
  • No trees needed: we can adapt to your ground.
  • 3 to 15 slacklines, depending on the space and the audience.
  • Qualified instructors.

Highline show and demo

Height
From 8 m
Length
20 to 800 m

A crossing above the village square, freestyle highline, or a show created for your event: we adapt to what you have in mind!

A highline brings a fresh look to a familiar place, and sets young and old dreaming.

A qualified and insured team

Our whole team holds the CQP cordiste rope access qualification and the ISA (International Slackline Association) Rigger certification. Every service is covered by our insurance.
